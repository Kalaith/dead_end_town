# Named survivors and item-led growth

## Small cooperative cast

The user chose few, symbolically important survivors rather than a large colony. The earlier suggestion of eight to twelve is a possible full-game ceiling, not a confirmed target. Prototype three named survivors plus the protagonist; recruit infrequently enough that each addition changes the community.

The cast works together. Personality appears through clothing, background, useful contributions, and short contextual lines. Do not add morale bars, loyalty checks, betrayal plots, social disputes, or personal skill grinding.

Specialities are small automatic efficiencies or access to contextual knowledge. Essential progression must not depend on a randomly unavailable specialist. Tool and facility discoveries do the main unlocking; everyone can contribute to basic jobs.

## Paige: the defining transformation

The user wrote Page; the ongoing discussion and current request use Paige. Use Paige consistently as the working name and keep final spelling open.

| Stage | Physical discovery or action | Visible result | New capability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rescue | Clear the library threat and escort her out | Librarian clothing, improvised weapon | Available worker and squad member |
| Improvised defender | Recover a shield and usable melee weapon | Shield and practical padding | Block a doorway and protect allies |
| Restoration project | Recover an armour set and bring back welding/repair tools | Armour displayed at the workshop during restoration | Recipe for usable heavy protection |
| Iron-armoured defender | Restore and equip the set at an operational workshop | Recognisable Paige in full iron armour | Better protection and stagger resistance at a chokepoint |

The museum armour and restoration chain are illustrative content. They make the outcome tangible without requiring a level threshold, repeated sword attacks, or a predetermined knight class. Another survivor can use the same armour. Paige can use a shotgun if the player chooses.

Her full-armour milestone should culminate in an observable defensive moment. It is not invulnerability: flanking, a broken gate, or overwhelming numbers still matter. Keep her face, glasses, palette, or other identifying details readable through equipment changes where practical.

## Progression categories

**Supplies** are consumed: construction materials, food, ammunition, and later medicine or metal. **Equipment** is a physical loadout choice. **Key discoveries** unlock recipes or community capabilities. **Facilities** perform work while held and operational.

Use explicit rules for each reward. Welding tools recovered and deposited unlock the reinforcement recipe permanently; producing reinforcement still needs a working workshop. Losing the garage pauses that production but does not erase knowledge. A sword remains one physical item and cannot be equipped by two survivors at once.

## Survivor interface

Show name, portrait, current activity, assignment, equipment, health/recovery, and location. Let the player choose a primary job, weapon, protection, and squad membership. Show only relevant unlocked equipment and explain missing facility requirements.

No XP allocation screen is needed. Equipment can change role immediately when safely equipped. Proposed MVP: change loadouts at accessible settlement storage, preventing remote swaps from duplicated inventory. Capacity, off-hand rules, and heavy-armour tradeoffs need combat testing.

## Recruitment and presentation

Survivors are exploration rewards, not a recurring labour-generation system. Not every house contains someone. A rescue establishes the person in the world, removes the immediate danger, and returns them safely; claim ownership separately when appropriate.

Keep dialogue short and cooperative. A librarian's enthusiasm about finally finding a shield can make an equipment upgrade memorable without creating a relationship subsystem. Optional personal interests may point toward buildings, but do not become compulsory mood-management quests.


## Implemented loadout choices, 28 October 2026

The restored workshop exposes paused F loadouts for the player, Mara and rescued Paige. Changing equipment requires both player and wearer at the clear bench with a protected home route. The garage crowbar and restored sword, iron and shield are unique items; return an item before transferring it. Each person retains a basic bat when no recovered weapon is selected. No item is duplicated or remotely swapped.

Bat is fast and single-target; crowbar is slower and stronger with more knockback; restored sword reaches farther and can hit two valid enemies. Iron halves incoming damage and reduces speed by 20%; shield protection is independent and frontal. Any person can use either. This supplies equipment-led role choices without classes or XP.
